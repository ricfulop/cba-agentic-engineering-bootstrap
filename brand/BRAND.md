# MIT brand marks

Files in this directory are official MIT logo-2 RGB marks (mit-red, white,
black only). The PowerPoint file in `templates/` is the official MIT
Arial template.

These marks are for **MIT-affiliated academic use**. Do not
add dual logos, confidential FAR footers, or any third-party lockup.

The template and SVGs are MIT official property and are **not relicensed**
by this repository. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
